Privacy Policy: Kathala Muchatlu Tools

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

"Kathala Muchatlu Tools" is a private helper application used only by the owner of the Kathala Muchatlu YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account (home page). It is not available to the public.

What the app does

  • Reads statistics of our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account (views, watch time, reach, likes, comments counts, followers).
  • Publishes our own videos and captions to our own accounts, only after the owner approves them.

Data we access and why

  • Google / YouTube API data: channel and video statistics, and uploading our own videos. Used only to run and improve our own channel.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) data: Page and Instagram insights, and publishing our own Reels and videos.
  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or other users. We do not read private messages.

Storage and security

Statistics are saved as files on the owner's own computer. Access permissions (tokens) are kept in the operating system's protected credential store and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Google API data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time: Google account → Security → Third-party access, and Facebook → Settings → Business integrations. To have any stored statistics deleted, the owner deletes the local files; no copies exist elsewhere. If you believe the app holds data about you, contact us through the Kathala Muchatlu YouTube channel or Instagram @KathalaMuchatlu and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
